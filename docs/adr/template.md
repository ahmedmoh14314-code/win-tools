# ADR-NNNN: Title (a decision, not a topic)

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded by ADR-NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** product owner (date)
- **Related requirements:** IDs from `docs/product/requirements.json`

## Context
What forces are at play: product constraints, Windows platform facts (cite docs), risks, and what happens if we do nothing.

## Decision
One or two sentences in the active voice. If not yet decided, write "Not yet decided" and list the questions that must be answered.

## Alternatives considered
For each: what it is, why it was rejected or kept open, with evidence where it exists.

## Consequences
Positive, negative and neutral. Include what becomes harder, what must be maintained, and what is now ruled out.

## Evidence / experiments
Links to `docs/research/` files, spike directories, benchmark results. Material uncertainty is resolved by experiment, not opinion. Say explicitly if evidence is missing.

## Evaluator review
Date, reviewer brief used, findings and their outcomes (see `docs/engineering/evaluator-pattern.md`). An ADR cannot move to Accepted without this section.
